Chapter 12

Relevant Memory Selection and Serialization

Section 12.3

Serialization Order and Structure

The relevant memory state is distributed and parallel, while Transformer consumption is sequential. Serialization therefore preserves semantic structure through ordering rather than through a newly invented vector space.

  • Dialogue identity and active objective, when required by the interface.
  • Prior Dialogue content needed to resolve current references.
  • Most strongly matched LTM1 atoms and their active contexts.
  • Linked LTM2 episodes, compositions, scenarios, and expected transitions.
  • Competing alternatives, uncertainty, and provenance where they affect interpretation.
  • An explicit completion boundary.

Preserve relations

Serialization must preserve which elements are contexts, relations, alternatives, causes, consequences, or temporal successors. A flat unordered bag of activations is insufficient.

Implementation freedom

The exact ordering policy may be learned, deterministic, or hybrid, but it must produce representations consumable by the selected Transformer interface.
