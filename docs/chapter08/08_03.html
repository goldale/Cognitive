Chapter 8

Planning and Action Selection

Section 8.3

The Next-Step Criterion

The best next step is not necessarily the change with the largest immediate benchmark gain. It is the change that creates a fully working system, produces the highest utility for foreseeable cost, and expands the space of later improvements.

Priority = PracticalUtility × FutureOptionValue / (Cost × Risk × Irreversibility)

This criterion favors persistent Research State, consolidation infrastructure, and hybrid orchestration because each improves current work while generating data for later architecture.

A speculative integrated memory may have larger theoretical value but lower current priority when it cannot yet be evaluated or repaired.
