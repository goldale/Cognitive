Chapter 24

Documentation Architecture

Section 24.2

Typed Knowledge Objects

Typed knowledge objects make epistemic and normative status explicit.

IdentifierTypePurpose
RSResearch SessionStructured records of research questions, constraints, hypotheses, and unresolved outcomes
RNResearch NoteHypotheses and open research directions
ANArchitectural NoteRationale and interpretation of architectural decisions
INImplementation NoteReference engineering approaches and non-normative technology choices
HPHistorical PerspectiveHistorical analogies and motivating examples without claims of historical proof
MNMargin NoteDeferred alternatives and side observations preserved outside the canonical architecture

DA-0003 — Typed Knowledge Objects

Every contextual knowledge object has a declared type, stable identifier, title, status, canonical location, and linkable HTML anchor.

Normative boundary

RS, RN, IN, HP, and MN objects do not change architectural contracts unless a separate canonical architectural revision explicitly adopts their conclusions.
