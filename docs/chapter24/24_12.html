Section 24.12

RS-0010 — Logical Episode Segmentation

RS-0010 — Logical Episode Segmentation

Research Session. Status: Open. Priority: High.

Research question

How should the Transformer identify Logical Episode start, continuation, interruption, resumption, and end within a continuous observation stream so that complete semantic episodes can be transferred into LTM2 within the single physical LTM?

  • Time is not explicitly stored inside events.
  • STM contains no explicit episode markers unless such information is produced by the cognitive process.
  • LTM2 must not receive arbitrary STM windows.

ARCHITECTURAL DECISION REQUIRED

Define the representation and mechanism used to determine Logical Episode transitions, including whether the decision is causal, retrospective, or hybrid, and how uncertain or revised episode hypotheses are represented.

  • Define how an episode remains open while additional evidence arrives.
  • Define evidence for logical start, continuation, interruption, resumption, and logical end.
  • Handle nested, overlapping, interleaved, and uncertain episodes.
  • Specify revision semantics for premature or false segmentation decisions.
  • Define the transfer contract that preserves a completed sequence until LTM2 accepts it.
  • Develop training objectives and measurable validation criteria.

Working hypothesis

A Transformer maintains one or more continuously updated episode hypotheses over the semantic interpretation of the observation stream. It emits a completed Logical Episode only when semantic closure exceeds a defined confidence threshold and later evidence is unlikely to change the selected interval. This is a research hypothesis, not yet a canonical implementation.

  • Can an episode transition be emitted online, or must every episode allow a retrospective correction interval?
  • How are simultaneous real-world processes separated when their observations are interleaved in one stream?
  • Should episode transitions be represented as discrete events, probability distributions, or state transitions?
  • What prevents trivial segmentation into one-event episodes or one unbounded episode?
