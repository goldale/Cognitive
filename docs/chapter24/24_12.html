Section 24.12

RS-0010 — D-Context Lifecycle

RS-0010 — D-Context Lifecycle

Research Session. Status: Open. Priority: High.

Research question

How should D-Context start, continuation, interruption, resumption, replacement, and end be determined within continuous observation streams while D-Context remains solely inside STM within Associative Memory?

  • Time is not explicitly stored inside events.
  • D-Context owns episode membership and boundary semantics inside STM.
  • LTM2 must not receive arbitrary STM windows.

ARCHITECTURAL DECISION REQUIRED

Define the representation and mechanism used to determine D-Context lifecycle transitions, including whether the decision is causal, retrospective, or hybrid, and how uncertain or revised context hypotheses are represented.

  • Define how D-Context keeps an episode open while additional evidence arrives.
  • Define how D-Context represents evidence for start, continuation, interruption, resumption, and end.
  • Define how D-Context handles nested, overlapping, interleaved, and uncertain episodes.
  • Specify revision semantics for premature or false segmentation decisions.
  • Define how ordered observations are retained until D-Context completes an episode representation and LTM2 accepts it.
  • Develop training objectives and measurable validation criteria.

Working hypothesis

The system may maintain one or more continuously updated context hypotheses over the semantic interpretation of observation streams and use them to revise D-Context when sufficient evidence is available. This is a research hypothesis, not yet a canonical implementation.

  • Can an episode transition be emitted online, or must every episode allow a retrospective correction interval?
  • How are simultaneous real-world processes separated when their observations are interleaved in one stream?
  • Should episode transitions be represented as discrete events, probability distributions, or state transitions?
  • What prevents trivial segmentation into one-event episodes or one unbounded episode?
