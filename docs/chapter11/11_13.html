Chapter 11

Associative Memory

Section 11.13

Logical Episode Consolidation Boundary

Logical Episode boundaries may be supplied by the source, implied by the activation stream, or inferred from combined context.

ARCHITECTURAL DECISION REQUIRED

Define which mechanism determines Logical Episode start, continuation, interruption, resumption, and end when these are not supplied explicitly by the source. The current canonical architecture does not assign this responsibility to a dedicated physical component.

Completed Logical Episodes are integrated into Associative Memory through the latent UPDATE interaction. Their internal order and causal hypotheses are represented by the resulting LTM structures and edge lists.
