Chapter 11

Associative Memory

Section 11.13

Logical Episode Consolidation Boundary

Logical Episode boundaries may be supplied by the source, implied by the activation stream, or inferred by Transformer processing from combined context.

No boundary-detection module

Boundary inference is an operation of the Transformer Cognitive Core over the active state. The architecture does not define a separate sequencing or boundary-detection component.

Completed Logical Episodes are integrated into Associative Memory through the latent UPDATE interaction. Their internal order and causal hypotheses are represented by the resulting LTM structures and edge lists.
