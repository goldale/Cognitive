Chapter 11

Canonical Memory Representation

Section 11.5

Sparse Representation Optimization

After decorrelation, a further rotation may seek sparse typical representations in which simple concepts activate relatively few coordinates. Statistical independence and semantic interpretability are separate objectives and must not be conflated.

Representation Optimization changes the coordinate system, not the accumulated knowledge or memory graph topology. It is therefore distinct from UPDATE and from sleep-like structural reorganization.
