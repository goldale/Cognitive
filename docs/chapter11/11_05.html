Chapter 11

Canonical Memory Representation

Section 11.5

Self-Organizing Semantic Coordinate System

Motivation

The semantic coordinate system of Associative Memory is not fixed in advance. It emerges gradually as the cognitive system accumulates experience.

During continuous learning, different semantic dimensions may begin to encode partially overlapping properties. This creates representational redundancy, increases statistical correlation between dimensions, and reduces the efficiency of the latent semantic space.

The purpose of semantic coordinate optimization is not merely to eliminate statistical correlation. Its broader objective is to maintain a stable, efficient, and minimally redundant semantic basis throughout the lifetime of the cognitive system.

Semantic coordinates should gradually evolve toward a representation in which each dimension describes a distinct semantic property with minimal overlap with other dimensions.

Stage 1 — Global Basis Initialization

After the Memory State has accumulated sufficient experience and reached an acceptable level of semantic stability, a one-time global optimization is performed.

  • Compute the correlation matrix of the semantic vector dimensions.
  • Construct an approximately orthogonal semantic coordinate system.
  • Transform the existing semantic representations into the resulting basis.

This operation establishes the initial canonical semantic coordinate system used by Associative Memory and the Transformer. Global basis initialization is performed only once during the lifetime of the cognitive system and is not repeated as new experience accumulates.

Stage 2 — Localized Semantic Basis Maintenance

As new experience is incorporated into Memory State, the statistical properties of the semantic coordinate system gradually evolve. This process may introduce new correlations between semantic dimensions that were previously approximately independent.

The existing semantic basis is therefore maintained through incremental localized optimization rather than repeated global orthogonalization.

  • Update the maintained correlation matrix.
  • Identify the pair of semantic dimensions with the strongest remaining correlation.
  • Select, from the remaining semantic coordinate axes, the one having the weakest statistical correlation with the selected semantic pair to serve as the reference rotation axis.
  • Determine the rotation angle that minimizes the correlation of the selected semantic pair while preserving the selected reference axis.
  • Apply the corresponding local rotation, modifying only the selected pair of semantic coordinates.
  • Immediately update the correlation matrix.

Each refinement step changes exactly two elements of the semantic vector. The selected reference axis and all remaining semantic coordinates remain unchanged.

The procedure may be repeated for additional coordinate pairs until the remaining correlations fall below the required threshold or the available optimization budget is exhausted.

Semantic Continuity and Transformer Adaptation

Restricting each refinement step to a localized transformation minimizes disruption to the established semantic basis. Only the selected pair of semantic dimensions is modified during a single optimization step. All remaining semantic coordinates preserve their semantic interpretation.

  • Previously learned representations remain valid for all unaffected dimensions.
  • Adaptation is limited to the modified semantic pair.
  • Semantic evolution becomes gradual rather than disruptive.
  • Computational cost and Transformer retraining effort are substantially reduced.

This localized optimization allows the semantic coordinate system to evolve continuously throughout the lifetime of the cognitive system while preserving semantic continuity and Transformer stability.

Semantic Drift

Continuous learning gradually changes the statistical distribution of semantic representations. This long-term evolution is referred to as semantic drift. Semantic drift is a natural consequence of incorporating new knowledge and refining existing semantic distinctions.

Localized semantic basis maintenance compensates for accumulated drift through small rotations. It preserves the established coordinate system wherever possible while correcting only dimensions that have developed significant redundancy.

The objective is not to maintain perfect orthogonality at every moment. The objective is to preserve a sufficiently independent, stable, and efficient semantic basis under continuous learning.

Relationship to Offline Consolidation

Semantic basis optimization is a form of structural learning. It is not performed during normal READ or UPDATE operations.

  • READ never modifies the semantic coordinate system.
  • UPDATE stores semantic representations using the currently active basis.
  • Online functional learning does not perform coordinate rotations.
  • Global basis initialization and localized basis maintenance are performed exclusively during Offline Consolidation (Sleep).

Offline Consolidation is responsible for coordinating the corresponding adaptation of the Transformer and persistent memory representations. A basis refinement must not become visible to normal online processing until the affected memory representations and Transformer interfaces are mutually consistent.

Semantic preservation invariant

Coordinate optimization may change the latent basis, but it must preserve represented knowledge and the strict separation of READ, UPDATE, and Offline Consolidation.

Design Rationale

The semantic coordinate system is treated as a long-lived internal representation rather than as a fixed mathematical structure.

  • An initially coherent semantic coordinate system.
  • Lifelong semantic self-organization.
  • Gradual reduction of representational redundancy.
  • Preservation of semantic continuity.
  • Localized and computationally efficient optimization.
  • Reduced adaptation requirements for the Transformer.
  • Avoidance of repeated disruptive global transformations.

The resulting semantic basis remains stable enough for reliable online reasoning while retaining the ability to adapt incrementally as the cognitive system acquires new experience.
