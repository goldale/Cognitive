Chapter 11

Associative Memory

Section 11.12

External Multimodal Input Flow

External multimodal observations enter the active processing path as Transformer-compatible activations. This section intentionally does not define an additional persistent architectural component between the external source and the active internal state.

ARCHITECTURAL DECISION REQUIRED

Define how incoming observations are retained and ordered before or during integration into the active internal state, including the lifetime of unconsumed observations and the behavior when multiple external streams progress concurrently.
