# Origin and Co-authorship

This project emerged from a prolonged research dialogue between:

- a human systems engineer with training in applied mathematics, phase-transition physics, Linux, and C++ software engineering; and
- an OpenAI language model acting as an iterative research partner, formalizer, critic, consolidator, and implementation tool.

Many hypotheses existed in partial form before the dialogue. Other ideas emerged only after repeated reformulation and conflict resolution. The resulting Research State and implementation are products of joint cognitive work. They are not a transcript and should not be interpreted as a conventional division between author and editor.
